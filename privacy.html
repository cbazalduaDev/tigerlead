Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Tiger Lead Studio ("the app") is a private tool used by one person, the Tiger Lead creator, to publish their own videos to their own accounts and to read their statistics.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google API Services): uploads videos and captions to the creator's channel and reads view counts and watch-time statistics for those videos.
  • TikTok: publishes videos to the creator's accounts and reads the creator's basic profile, follower count and the view, like, comment and share counts of their videos.

What the app stores

Login tokens and the IDs of published videos are stored only on the creator's own computer. The app does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other people, and it has no servers, analytics or advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
